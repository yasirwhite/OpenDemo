## How it was checked

- **Cuts.** A per-frame diff trace finds all 41 of the rebuild's cuts on the
  same frames as the reference's. The reference's only extra "cuts" are
  f205-f218, where its animated frieze (P13) changes every frame.
- **Matched frames.** 14 timestamps across all four sections were paired
  reference | rebuild (edge height, sag, sky and body colour, type size,
  baseline and bend).
- **Motion energy** (mean per-frame diff, reference / rebuild): s1 0.42 /
  0.28, s2 before the line 7.17 / 5.16, s2+s3 10.58 / 10.16, s4 1.97 / 1.91.
  The line section and the end card are within ~4%. The opening haze and the
  early plates move less, because generated textures are calmer than the
  reference's photos.
- **End pan.** The tracked horizon edge at f452-f500 is within 1 px of the
  reference, and the last frame lands on frame 1 (edge y 260 vs 257 at 960w).

## Caveats

- **Plates are stand-ins.** Colour, value, sky, edge and rhythm match, but
  generated noise, rings, cells and hatching are not macro photographs. The
  rebuild reads flatter and more graphic than the reference until real photos
  go in the 36 slots. Worst matches: P16 (thermal sky), P23 (lace), P34 (ink
  sketch) and P13 (the reference's animated frieze is slowly drifting shapes
  here).
- **Font.** Georgia stands in for the reference's book serif, sized by
  x-height. Bent words rotate glyphs along an SVG path instead of warping
  them.
- **Grain.** The grain is one soft-light layer. The reference's grain comes
  from its photos and varies from plate to plate.
- **Brand halo.** The reference account has 2.0M followers, so part of the
  engagement is reach. It was still the clear standout among short films.
- **No engine change beyond `--scale`.** A float edge case (203/25*1000 =
  8119.999…) would have started some scenes a frame late. The config handles
  it with `leadMs: 1` on every scene, and the line and title presets refuse
  to overlap.
- **Merge with maple (#3) and juniper (#2).** The style commit is maple's
  198b3db, cherry-picked. Whichever lands later gets trivial add/add
  conflicts in the presets index, `templates/index.json` and
  `references/references.json`.
- **Human eye wanted** on: whether the generated plates carry the film
  without photos, and the bent type on 13.3–16s.
