## How it was checked

- **Tracked curves, replayed.**
  - The lime key's face is tracked every frame: rise, sink, slide, step and
    drop.
  - The pill's top edge is tracked every frame through its rise and
    overshoot (cy 737 to a 333 peak to 361) and its drop and shrink (scale
    1 to 0.597).
  - The rebuild's pill track matches the reference's top edge to the 4 px
    sampling step on every frame checked (f78-f104, f154-f196).
- **Cuts.** The one hard cut lands on f521 in both films (diff 63.7 vs
  63.8). The only other large diffs in the rebuild are the wedge sweep
  (f174-f176), as in the reference. A one-frame pop at the end of the wedge
  was found and fixed: a triangle clip collapsed as the wedge opened toward
  180 deg.
- **Matched frames.** 60+ timestamps were paired reference | rebuild at
  matched size across five rounds. These were fixed between rounds:
  - wedge timing (2 frames early)
  - deletion start
  - the card's leftward collapse
  - the line's slide curve (an S, not an ease-out)
  - icon arrivals (3 frames early)
  - the hand-title weight and size
  - the pill riding through the cut
  - the end-card draw curve
  - the ribbon drawn over the keycap
- **Motion energy and luminance** (mean per-frame diff / mean luminance,
  reference / rebuild):

  | section | motion | luminance |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 keys | 1.90 / 2.01 | 233.2 / 231.4 |
  | pill drop + wedge | 5.31 / 5.22 | 210.4 / 208.1 |
  | composer | 0.87 / 0.61 | 197.9 / 197.9 |
  | line + icons | 0.64 / 0.58 | 241.2 / 239.1 |
  | title | 0.75 / 0.76 | 237.5 / 236.0 |

- **Comprehension.** A fresh haiku reader was shown 10 demo frames with no
  context. It explained the product back correctly: a desktop dictation app
  where you hold a key, talk, and clean text is typed into whatever app you
  are in.

## Caveats

- **Brand halo.** Granola is a known product, though the account is
  27.6k followers. The film's grammar (keycaps, a voice pill, dictation)
  suits input and shortcut features best. It is a narrower fit than a
  general launch template.
- **Fonts.** Georgia, Segoe UI, Ink Free and Consolas are Windows fonts. Off
  Windows, the hand title changes most.
- **Composer copy wraps** to "clean text" on line 2. Each field's README
  entry gives a length budget.
- **Wedge texture and collage prints are generated.** They read as torn
  paper and printed skirts, but flatter than the reference's scans.
- **Human eye wanted** on:
  - the wedge wipe (5.0-6.3s)
  - the hand title (14-17s)
  - whether the keycap legends ("Laurel / voice / dictation") read as a
    shortcut
