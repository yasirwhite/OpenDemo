## Runbook friction

1. One section-agent launch was interrupted at the tool-permission layer. Its
   files existed, but no report came back. The orchestrator recovered by
   verifying the files directly.
2. On Windows, Python's default codepage silently turned UTF-8 copy into
   mojibake (`Iâ€™m`) during assembly. Always open JSON with
   `encoding="utf-8"`.
3. Frame times that land exactly on integer-ms cut boundaries flip sides from
   float error in sequential renders. Agents floored starts to before the
   frame. A shared convention, starting a scene half a frame early, would help.
4. CPU: a 4-core machine was shared with another session. A full 90s render
   took ~3.5 min per pass (side-by-side and demo). Section agents reported
   occasional first-screenshot timeouts under load.
