## Caveats for the reviewer

- **The webcam tiles (0–7.5s) are drawn stand-ins:** room tone, a window
  and a head-and-shoulders silhouette. They read as mannequins next to real
  footage. They are `people[].photo` slots, and a real retarget should fill
  them.
- **Fonts.** Georgia stands in for the reference's text serif and Segoe UI
  for its geometric sans. The sans is narrower, so some lines wrap
  differently and the chat stack heights shift slightly. The bubbles are
  tracked to match width.
- **Sort beat (31.5–39s).** The camera was solved per frame. Notes that the
  reference hides mid-drag have hand-keyed paths, with timing ±2–4 frames.
  The vote stamp art is simplified.
- **Loop glyph.** S2 and S3 each built their own travelling-loop line;
  they look alike but are separate code (`WorkLoop` in shared and a local
  one in S3). The phase is a few frames off at the S2 erase.
- **The whips** are a speed-scaled vertical blur. The reference's streaks
  are more banded.
- **Gantt owners.** "Noor" and "Kit" and the milestone city "Portland" were
  invented by S4. They are fields.
- **Reaction emoji.** The tagline's reaction bubble uses an emoji (👏). It
  depends on Segoe UI Emoji being present.
- **Expected merge conflicts** with open PRs #7 (hazel), #11 (linden) and
  #12 (spruce). They are all additive, in the shared registries:
  - `src/lib/textcards/src/presets/index.jsx` (import plus spread)
  - `templates/index.json`
  - `references/references.json`
  - `references/launch-scouting.json`

  Keep both sides.

## Runbook friction

1. The fresh clone has no husky hooks. `biome check` was run by hand: the
   cypress files and registry edits are clean. `references/*.json` show
   CRLF-only diffs in the working tree, from `core.autocrlf`.
2. `render.mjs` has no GPU-off flags and writes to a shared `dist/`. A
   private builder (esbuild plus a config-injection plugin) and renderer in
   the night's scratch let four agents build in parallel safely. They are
   worth upstreaming as `build.mjs --out` and `render.mjs --chrome-flags`.
3. On this shell, bash heredocs collapse `\\`. Write regexes in scripts
   without double backslashes.
