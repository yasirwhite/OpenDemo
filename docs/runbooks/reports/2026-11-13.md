## How it was checked

- **Passes.** Five still-frame passes (16–45 frames each, paired with the
  reference at matched frames) and two full renders. Fixes along the way:
  type sizes (fitted by line width), the aura ring colours (sampled down
  the reference's centre column), the glow (rebuilt from the rect's
  distance field after box-shadow showed nine-patch seams), the closing
  aura's stacking, the bloom timing, and four beat boundaries moved 1–2
  frames onto the reference's cuts.
- **Cuts.** A per-frame diff trace (160x90) on both films: 15 of the
  reference's 21 detected cuts land within one frame in the rebuild. Misses:
  the invite dialog (f1077, ours f1080), the toggle glow (f1972, ours too
  low-contrast to register), the three big-word swaps (f1630/1639/1648) and
  the url (f2352). The last four are on the right frames but have the same
  colours either side, so the trace does not register them.

  reference vs rebuild):

## Caveats

- **Drawn product.** The reference's real app captures, laptop photos and
  portrait photography are drawn stand-ins (sites, editor, dashboards,
  avatars). The film reads as a product film without footage, but it is
  flatter than the reference until recordings go into the window slots.
- **Font.** Segoe UI stands in for the reference's grotesk; it is wider,
  so tracking is pulled in and sizes were fitted by line width.
- **Less motion than the reference** (~60%, table above). A few of the
  reference's morphs are hard cuts here: the orbs-to-big-word star wipe
  (f312), the white flood into cream (f372–378), the letter-to-flower step
  (f546) and the pill-window close-up (f835). The bloom into the site wall
  is the roughest transition.
- **Softness.** The auras and the glow are gradient-exact; the reference's
  are a little softer and its grain coarser on dark grounds.
- **Copy** is new but follows the reference's structure beat for beat
  ("Fewer than 1% of people { can write code } but now anyone can",
  "Quicker than you'd think", "publish instantly what you made on your own
  domain").
- **Merge conflicts expected with #7 (hazel), #10 (laurel), #11 (linden):**
  all four add a spread + import to `src/lib/textcards/src/presets/index.jsx`
  and append to `templates/index.json`, `references/references.json` and
  `references/launch-scouting.json`. Trivial add/add conflicts; keep both
  sides.
- **Rendering speed:** a React style warning (font shorthand mixed with
  lineHeight) made the first full render crawl at ~1 fps; fixed by using
  longhand font properties. Worth knowing for other presets.
- **Human eye wanted** on the bloom-into-wall transition (18.0–21.4s) and
  on whether the drawn sites on the laptops are good enough as defaults.
