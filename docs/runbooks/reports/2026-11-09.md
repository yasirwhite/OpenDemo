## Reference

- **QuiverAI, "Introducing Arrow 2"**, posted 2026-09-16 by @QuiverAI:
  https://x.com/QuiverAI/status/2100295136261349802
- **Engagement** (fxtwitter, re-checked 2026-11-09): 2,366,346 views, 11,184
  likes, 834 reposts and 14,079 bookmarks on a 24,745-follower account, about
  96x the follower count. Okara has more reach (14.1M views, 27k likes) but is
  ~95% raw capture; Endex has 10.8M views and 8k likes with ~30% founder
  footage. Arrow 2 is the strongest accepted, unbuilt film with a drawn spine.
- **Source:** 43.84s, 1280x720 @25fps, the best variant on X. Rebuilt on its
  own 25fps clock. The media was already on disk from the 2026-11-08 run, so
  nothing new was downloaded.
- **Why this one.** The brief asked for drawn, motion-designed beats after
  rowan's reliance on stand-ins for screen recordings. Almost every beat here
  can be drawn: line art, halftone, a word drum, an easing curve, letterforms,
  outlines, mosaics. Okara is ~95% raw capture and Endex is ~30% founder
  footage, so neither was picked. The look (paper white, cyan line art, one sky
  field) differs from all nine templates, including maple, which is
  black-and-white hard cuts on a single mark from the same company.

## How it was checked

- **Cuts.** Per-frame diff trace on both films (160x90):
  - Every cut in the reference has a rebuild cut within one frame, except f617
    (the two cap cuts differ too little to pass the threshold here) and f759
    (the reference's generation ground; ours lands on f757 as the panel cuts).
  - The rebuild has three extra peaks: f251 (the drum stop change), f330 (the
    dissolve to the sky field begins slightly stronger) and f757 (the panel
    swap, which is softer in the reference).
- **Matched frames.** 47 timestamps paired reference | rebuild at matched
  size across the iterations. These match within a few px: the sky pill and curves, the panel edge,
  the split edges and window, the drum strip and tiles, the code panel, the
  word-draw framing, the strip, the table layout, the composer, the picture
  block, the claims and the wordmark.

## Caveats

- **Drawn stand-ins for photography.** Mockups and stamps are flat drawn
  scenes, so the two montages read as graphics until real photos fill the 20
  slots. This is the biggest gap against the reference.
- **Art is simpler than the reference's generated work.** Fewer strokes in the
  line art, simpler motifs and fewer tiles in the table's mosaic.
- **Fonts.** System fonts only: Segoe UI, Georgia Italic, Bahnschrift, Impact,
  Consolas. The letter-draw word depends on Georgia Italic being installed.
- **Same company as maple.** Maple came from this company's earlier film. The
  two share a vector-tool subject but not a look. Human eye wanted on whether
  the gallery wants both.
- **Working-tree line endings.** `references/*.json`, `templates/index.json`
  and the presets registry were normalised to LF so `biome check` passes. Git
  sees no line-ending change from that.
- **Human eye wanted** on the drawn mockups (23–26s) and the table mosaic
  (26–28.6s).
