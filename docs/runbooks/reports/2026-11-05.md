## Caveats

- **Fonts.** System fonts only: Arial or Segoe UI for the prompt and UI
  face, Consolas for the code and url, Century Gothic for the lockup.
  Sizes are matched on x-height or cap height.
- **Sketch photo drawn.** The opening is a drawn pencil sketch on drawn paper
  (simpler desk grain, pen and pencil texture). The `sketch-photo` slot is
  available (`photo: "slot"`, `src`).
- **Mockup slots.** The eight montage shots are flat tonal washes with corner
  captions until real photos are supplied, so s5's frame difference is the
  largest of the film (mean 13.7 vs 1.8–6.1 for the other sections).
- **Blackletter drawn.** No blackletter is installed. The flash's
  blackletter "Lorem" is drawn from broad-nib strokes, and its "L" is simpler
  than the reference's.
- **Gradients approximate.** The full-bleed field is a sampled grid and the
  cards are blob specs. Hue and layout match, but fold lines and swoosh edges
  are softer. Flipping cards show faint 8-bit banding for 1–2 frames.
- **Marks differ by design.** The marks use Maple's arrowhead knockout and
  samara blades, so the inner strokes, fillet dots and knockout comets follow
  a different path from the reference's.
- **Cursor exit f211.** Ours trails the reference's by a few px as it leaves
  the frame upward (s2 measured ≤10 px). Not visible at speed.
- **Merge with juniper.** The style commit is byte-identical to juniper's
  (17a96c9) and so is the `--scale` hunk. Whichever lands second will still
  hit trivial textual conflicts where both add an entry:
  - the presets import and spread in `src/lib/textcards/src/presets/index.jsx`
  - the `templates/index.json` entry
  - the `references/references.json` entry
- **Shared follow-ups not applied** (each would change rendered frames or
  rework working section code):
  - a `MapleMark` option for knockout rounding independent of the hexagon's,
    which would retire s5's `BladesMark`
  - moving s5's corner caption onto `ImageSlot labelPosition`
  - folding s4's `CARD_ART` into the shared `GRADIENTS`
- **Human eye wanted** on: the opening sketch (0–1s), the mockup montage
  (17–19s) and the font flash (22–23s).
