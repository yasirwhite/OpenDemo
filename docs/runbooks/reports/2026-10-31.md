## Runbook friction

1. **Slug collisions are silent.** Two sections picked the same preset slug
   twice (`sequoia-chat-reply`, `sequoia-circle-wipe`), and the later spread
   overrode the earlier one with no warning. The agents caught it and renamed.
   A duplicate-slug check in the registry would help.
2. **Seams on integer ms miss frames.** At 29.97/30fps most frame times are
   not integer ms. A seam at 66567 left frame 1997 (66566.67) to nobody,
   because the presets' own `live()` check ignores `leadMs`. Put seams at frame
   times, or half a frame early.
3. **Agents still ran `cd`.** Four of seven section agents used one `cd` inside
   a heredoc despite the brief. Nothing was denied or broken this time.
4. **CPU.** Seven section agents ran on 4 cores alongside the other session.
   Sections took 30–43 min. The full side-by-side took 4 min and the demo
   4.5 min. There were no collisions: the worktree and private stages kept
   every build separate.

## Ship checklist

`.husky/_`, which husky generates only in the main checkout. A plain
`git commit` in the worktree therefore runs no hook. Committing that way would
