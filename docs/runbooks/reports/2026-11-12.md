## Shipped (PR, unmerged)

- **linden**: a 25s gallery-wall film for an image or video model.
  - One card alone on black drifts toward camera while the wall fades up
    behind it in a widening pool of light.
  - The card sinks back into its slot and the wall lights to full.
  - The camera pushes, rolls and yaws onto a focus card, pulls back across
    the wall, and dives into the black gutter beside the first card.
  - Three white lines follow, each fading in over 16 frames and cutting out
    hard: "Linden Studio 1.0", "Type a line, get a film", and the mark with
    "linden.studio".
  - **Placeholder product:** Linden Studio 1.0. Nothing from the reference is
    used: no name, mark, copy, pictures or clips. The cards carry drawn
    placeholder art coloured from the reference's card palette.
  - **Scenes and slots:** 2 presets (`linden-wall`, `linden-line`) and 4
    scenes. **34 image slots**: `hero`, `focus`, 31 `media` and `markSrc`.
    Image slots are nested fields, so they don't touch the scene-level `src`
    path; a slot test with real JPGs rendered without stalling. No engine
    change was needed.
  - `template.json`: 1920x1080 @30fps, authored on the 1280x720 stage:
    `render.mjs --fps 30 --scale 1.5`.

## How it was checked

- **The camera is solved, not eyeballed.** The wall is a plane, so each frame
  is a homography:
  - It was found with SIFT and RANSAC: chained f114-f260, from a direct
    f250 match for f261-f360, direct against f114 for f361-f545, and chained
    from f530 to the end.
  - The pure chain drifted 13-20 wall units after f270. Direct matches
    caught it, and the fix was confirmed on the gutter beside the hero
    (stage x): f540 697-759 measured vs 700-762 solved; f550 653-783 vs
    657-783; f555 579-845 vs 586-844.
  - Warp overlays of f114 onto f280-f530 line up card for card.
- **The opening is tracked separately.** The card's width comes from
  template matching (196.5 at f1, 276 at f94, 80 at f110). The wall behind
  it comes from SIFT with the card masked out (scale 0.935-1.006). The pool
  of light comes from ring-by-ring brightness ratios.
- **Lines.** Fade frames, cut frames, baseline and cap height were measured
  from luminance.
- **Matched frames.** 35+ timestamps were paired reference | rebuild over
  four rounds. These were fixed between rounds:
  - the dive's last frame (x2.4 push off f557)
  - the art's saturation and variety
  - one duplicated mascot
  - the hero's neighbour's art
- **Motion energy and luminance** (mean per-frame diff / mean luminance,
  reference / rebuild):

  | section | motion | luminance |
  |---|---|---|
  | A one card | 0.87 / 0.34 | 19.1 / 22.7 |
  | B sink | 6.66 / 4.87 | 30.7 / 34.8 |
  | C push | 14.81 / 6.89 | 56.4 / 64.2 |
  | D pull | 16.51 / 7.41 | 57.9 / 60.0 |
  | E dive | 25.33 / 12.04 | 68.8 / 73.2 |
  | lines | 0.34 / 0.37 | 1.9 / 1.7 |

  Motion peaks land on the same frames (f190, f265, f366). Rebuild motion
  energy is about half the reference's because flat drawn cards carry less
  texture than playing clips; the camera path is the same.
- **Comprehension.** A fresh haiku reader saw 12 frames with no context. It
  explained the product back as a tool that turns a line of text into
  generated pictures and short films, shown as a wall of outputs.

## Caveats

- **Brand halo.** @xai has 2.1M followers. The craft was judged on its own,
  but the engagement is not purely film-borne.
- **Cards are stills.** The reference's cards are playing clips; here they
  are drawn art with a slow drift, or images with a 6% push. Video slots
  are a possible follow-up.
- **No motion blur** on the three fast whips or the sink.
- **The placeholder art is flat vector work.** It reads as generated output,
  but it is simpler than real outputs. The film is meant to be filled with
  real ones.
- **The last line adds a mark.** The reference ends on a bare address.
- **Registry conflicts.** Expect small add/add conflicts with hazel (#7) and
  laurel (#10) in `templates/index.json`, `references/*.json` and the preset
  registry, whichever lands later.
- **Human eye wanted** on:
  - the opening pool of light (0-3.3s)
  - whether the drawn art is good enough as default placeholders
