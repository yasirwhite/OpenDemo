## Assembly

- **Seams.** Stills one frame either side of every seam (3933, 7800, 25100,
  36133, 47066) needed no fixes. 25100 is pixel-identical on both sides.
- **Fixes after three full side-by-side renders**, all in the scene JSON
  except one opt-in preset parameter:
  - **S5 app launch** started ~2 frames early. `launchAtMs` moved to f1243.
  - **S5 push-in blur** (f1140–f1150) was isotropic and nearly sharp, where
    the reference smears radially. Added `zoomStreak` and `zoomShutter` to
    `juniper-builder-window` (copies averaged over the zoom travel); the
    scene uses 10. The default path is unchanged.
  - **S6 grey-to-ink ramps** on the claim and the end card reached ink ~2
    frames early. The fades went from 300 to 360 ms and from 600 to 750 ms,
    and now track within ~1 frame.
- **Ghost-edge hazard.** I rendered 70 frames (at 720p) and 23 frames (at
  1080p) across every moving beat, in sequence and solo. The only differences
  are 1-px anti-aliasing on current edges and sub-pixel text placement. There
  are no ghost edges of earlier frames.
- **Shared cleanup.** `UltraWatch` / `ULTRA` moved from `juniper-s3.jsx` into
  `juniper-shared.jsx`, and s3 and s4 now import it from there. All 1800
  frames hash identical before and after the change, re-checked against the
  final source.

## Caveats

- **Font.** Segoe UI stands in for SF. Long words run wider, ascenders run
  taller, and the end-card URL reads heavier than the reference.
- **Slots are flat faces.** Every app screen is a labelled flat face tinted
  like the original footage. The side-by-side looks emptier than the
  reference wherever footage moved.
- **Engine change.** `render.mjs --scale` is new, and the 1080p demo depends
  on it. It needs review.
- **Shared-vocabulary follow-ups (not applied, because each would change
  rendered frames):**
  - s3/s2's measured phone rim (`RimProfile`, `RIM` colours) differs from the
    shared `PhoneFrame` rim.
  - s4 drew local tablet, TV and headset frames because the shared frames
    carry drop shadows and a different tablet ring. s6 uses the shared ones.
  - The per-section table helpers (`tab` / `lut` / `MotionBlur`) are
    duplicated across sections.
- **Biome.** Everything under `templates/juniper/` is clean. The failures in
  `src/lib/textcards/src/presets/index.jsx` (organizeImports + format),
  `render.mjs`, `templates/index.json` and `references/*.json` are all
  pre-existing at HEAD. These files are 2-space indented, and the new entries
  follow their existing style.
- **Scratch edits.** The S5/S6 scene fixes live in each section's scene
  files. The sections' `gen.py` generators were not updated, so re-running
  them would undo the fixes.
- **Human eye wanted** on: the push-in at 38.0–38.4s, the app launch at
  41.4–41.6s, and the bento living art at 50–55s.
