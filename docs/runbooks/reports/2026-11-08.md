## How it was checked

- **Camera.** The window's edges were tracked every 5th frame of the
  reference. There are two linear centred pushes:
  - take A: window height 946 + 0.1954 px/frame, f75–f1037
  - take B: 946 + 0.1218 px/frame, f1041–f2189

  They run straight through every cut and dip. The rebuild replays them as
  keys, so the take swaps sit on one continuous move as they do in the
  reference.
- **Cuts and dips.** A per-frame diff trace (160x90) was run on both films:
  - Every cut lands on the reference's frame: f30, f75, f119, f1144, f1245,
    f1304, f1398, f1486, f1866, f1981, f2057, f2075 and f2190. The first
    render had five of them one frame late (rounding), and that was fixed
    before the final render.
  - The four dips reach black on the reference's frames: f1041, f1556,
    f1657 and f1724.
- **Matched frames.** 40 timestamps were paired reference | rebuild at
  matched size. These match the reference's geometry within a few px:
  - window, artboard, board grids and phone row
  - terminal and browser windows
  - caption positions and the end-card lines

## Caveats

- **The reference is mostly screen capture.** The template's value is the
  stage (backdrop, camera, cut rhythm, dips, captions, end card) plus drawn
  stand-ins. It reads best with real recordings in the 22 window slots. That
  is the call this night made, and it needs a human eye: is a
  window-recording template what the gallery wants?
- **Interiors are drawn approximations.** Panel layouts and positions follow
  the reference, but the UI density, photography and real cursor jitter do
  not. The mesh gradient is layered radial gradients.
- **Fonts.** Only Segoe UI and Consolas are installed on this machine. They
  stand in for the reference's grotesk and its lighter mono.
- **Caption copy.** The second caption was shortened to "Components with
  slots" to keep each caption one short outcome phrase.
- **16:9 vs the reference's 1812x1080.** The rebuild shows 54 px more
  backdrop on each side. In the side-by-side the reference is padded black
  there.
- **Working-tree line endings.** `references/*.json` and the presets
  registry were checked out with CRLF (autocrlf). They were normalised to LF
  in the working tree so `biome check` passes. Git sees no change from that,
  and the index stays LF.
- **Human eye wanted** on:
  - the build beat (4–14.6s)
  - the take swaps (14.6–34.6s), which are subtler than the reference's
  - the design-system zooms (57–69s)
