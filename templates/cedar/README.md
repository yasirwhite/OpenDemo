# cedar

The first OpenDemo template: a ~36-second kinetic-typography launch film with
two product slots, measured frame-by-frame off a real launch film (the "mem"
reference — see `src/lib/textcards/` for the measurements).

- `template.json` — the full film. Copy is placeholder ("Recall", a fictional
  note-keeping app); **timings, colours, easings and grounds are the template**
  and should survive retargeting untouched.
- Two `product-slot` scenes ship empty and render labelled placeholder boxes.
- Music grid: 116 BPM, cuts on downbeats — see `docs/music-sync.md`.

To retarget it to a real product, follow `templates/README.md`.
